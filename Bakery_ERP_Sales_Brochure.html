Rozana ki dikkat — pehchaan lo

Har bakery mein yeh problems common hain. System inhi ko theek karta hai.

1. Stock ka andaza

“Maida kitna bacha?” register pe likha hota hai, lekin asli stock alag hota hai. Andaza pe kaam — nuksaan pakka.

2. Expiry / Barbaadi

Purani cream, expired item, kharab maal — pata hi nahi chalta kitna paisa barbaad hua. Wastage chhupi rehti hai.

3. Order miss

WhatsApp pe cake order aaya, diary mein likha, delivery din bhool gaye. Customer naraz. Izzat aur business dono nuqsaan.

4. Cash leakage

Counter pe sale hui, shift band hua — opening aur closing match nahi. Paisa kahan gaya? Clear jawab nahi.

5. Supplier hisaab

Kaunsa vendor kitna dena hai? Bill kab aaya? Partial payment? Notebook mein uljhan. Hisaab ghalat = tension zyada.

6. Multi-branch confusion

Gulberg mein stock kam, DHA mein zyada — transfer ka record nahi. Shakhein alag, control nahi.

Bakery ERP kya karta hai?

Yeh software aapki bakery ki poori chain sambhalta hai — kacha maal (ingredients) se le kar counter sale, cake order, delivery, wasooli aur kharche tak. Har cheez ek jagah, clear, aur PKR mein.

01

Subah bake, din bhar sale, shaam stock check — har step system pe. Kaam easy, ghalti kam.

02

Paisa safe

Cash drawer, wasooli, bills, expenses — har rupay ka track. Leakage band.

03

Growth ready

Ek shop ho ya kai branches — same system, same control. Barhao bina chaos ke.

Socho jaise bakery ke alag departments — store, kitchen, counter, order desk, accounts — lekin sab ek hi computer / tablet system mein connected.

2

6

7

04
Fayda seedha — owner ke liye

Paisa, control, sukoon

Is system se aapko kya milta hai — clear words mein.

Operational fayde

  • Stock hamesha clear — kis branch mein kya hai
  • Expiry pehle use — barbaadi kam
  • Bake cost pata — nuskha + quantity se
  • Counter sale tez — stock khud update
  • Cake / WhatsApp orders miss nahi hote
  • Return / exchange ka proper record
  • Staff sirf apna module dekhe — control aapka

Financial fayde

Copy register ki jagah digital hisaab — har rupay ka rasta clear.

  • Cash drawer opening / closing match
  • Wasooli (customer payment) track
  • Supplier bills aur partial payments
  • Rent, bill, daily kharcha record
  • Bank + cash accounts ek jagah
  • COD outstanding nazar aaye

Asaan si baat

Agar aap abhi register, WhatsApp chats aur “yaad rakho” pe chal rahe ho — to har mahine chhupa nuksaan ho raha hai: gum stock, miss order, cash mismatch, expired maal. Bakery ERP yeh sab ek flow mein band karta hai. Seekhna mushkil nahi — screens simple hain, Urdu labels bhi system mein hain (Khareedari, Paidaawar, Wasooli, Barbaadi…).
